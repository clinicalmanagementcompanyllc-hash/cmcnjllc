Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and clinical lab certification pathways.

What services does CMCNJ provide for International Medical Graduates?

▼

CMCNJ helps IMGs navigate U.S. healthcare careers through eligibility consultations, Clinical Laboratory Scientist guidance, ASCP certification support, alternative career counseling, and personalized coaching.

How can an International Medical Graduate request an eligibility consultation?

▼

Fill out the intake form on the home page. We assess your credentials and recommend U.S. career pathways within 3-5 business days.

Can International Medical Graduates become Clinical Laboratory Scientists?

▼

Yes. IMGs can become Clinical Laboratory Scientists by obtaining ASCP certification, gaining U.S. clinical lab experience, meeting state licensure requirements, and optionally enrolling in bridging programs.

What are alternative careers for unmatched IMGs?

▼

Alternative careers include Clinical Laboratory Scientist, Physician Assistant, Nursing, Healthcare Administration, Clinical Research Coordinator, and Medical Education/Tutoring.

How can FMGs gain U.S. clinical laboratory experience?

▼

Experience can be gained via externships, internships, volunteer research labs, bridging programs, or supervised lab assistant positions.

What is the fastest non-residency healthcare career for IMGs?

▼

Fastest pathways include Clinical Laboratory Scientist (6–12 months), Research Coordinator (3–6 months), Medical Education/Tutoring (immediate), and Healthcare Administration (<1 year).
